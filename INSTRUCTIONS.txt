HOW TO RUN THE EXPERIMENT (about 30-40 minutes, most of it reboots)

Before every measurement:
- close all programs (browser, Discord, Steam, games);
- do not touch the computer while it is measuring;
- after the computer starts, wait 2-3 minutes so that Windows settles down.
One measurement takes about 5 minutes. At the end the window shows "Done": press any key.

STEP 1. Measure WITH mitigations.
  Double-click 1_measure_ON.bat

STEP 2. Turn mitigations off.
  Right-click 2_mitigations_OFF_admin.bat and choose "Run as administrator".
  Then REBOOT the computer.

STEP 3. Measure WITHOUT mitigations.
  Double-click 3_measure_OFF.bat

STEP 4. Restore mitigations (REQUIRED).
  Right-click 4_mitigations_RESTORE_admin.bat and choose "Run as administrator".
  Then REBOOT the computer.

STEP 5. One more measurement WITH mitigations (noise check).
  Double-click 1_measure_ON.bat again.

STEP 6. Compute the results.
  Double-click 5_analyze.bat
  The results appear in the results folder: the table summary.md and the chart slowdown.svg.

If the window says "STOP: you said --condition ...", you forgot to reboot after
step 2 or step 4. Nothing was recorded: reboot and run the step again.

If a measurement was interrupted (window closed, power cut), simply run that step again.
The analysis skips the interrupted run by itself and prints SKIPPED.

After step 3 the mitigations are off and the computer is less protected. Do not browse
the internet in this state: go straight to step 4. The window after step 3 and the
analysis (step 6) will remind you if the mitigations are still off.

Do not delete files from the results folder: they are the raw data published with the study.
